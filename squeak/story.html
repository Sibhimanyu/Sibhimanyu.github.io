The story so far

From a pager to a flock.

Squeak began as a fork of someone else's small Telegram bridge. Eight months and 1,922 commits later it runs your agents, tells you when one needs you, and lets you share the work.

  7. Oct 17 – Nov 7 2026Retired

    A detour we retired

    For a few weeks Squeak answered Conductor chats through a relay we built by reverse engineering Conductor. It worked, but it was someone else's app. We retired it in November, sealed it away, and built Squeak's own engine from scratch instead.

  8. Nov 8 – 9 2026

    Its own engine

    Squeak now runs Claude and Codex threads itself, in a session host that keeps going while the app updates. It brought approval cards, Ducklings (subagents), checkpoints, git and pull request actions, a terminal and ⌘K. The engine is Squeak's own, written clean-room.
