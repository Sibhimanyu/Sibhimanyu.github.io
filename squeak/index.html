Squeak for Mac, iPhone and web

Your agents. Your flock. In view.

Squeak runs your Claude and Codex threads on your Mac, tells you the moment one needs you, and lets you answer from anywhere.

01 / Threads

Claude and Codex, running on your Mac.

Squeak runs your agents itself. A thread is one agent on one task, working in its own stream: a git worktree, with its branch beside it. A separate session host keeps a running turn alive when the app closes or updates.

  1. 1

    Ponds, streams and threads

    Every project is a pond. Each thread works in a stream, so two threads never share a folder by accident.

  2. 2

    Answer where it asks

    Approvals and questions are cards in the thread. Allow once, allow for this thread, or type your own answer. Keys work too.

  3. 3

    Steer while it works

    Queue a message with Return, or steer the running turn with Command-Return. Ducklings and background work show live under “What it's doing”.

Every agent gets Squeak's tools

The workspace diff, review comments, notify, progress, and a way to list, read and message other threads, or start one. A thread can ask another for help.

Also in the window

  • Checkpoints and revert
  • Fork a thread
  • Edit an earlier message
  • Commit and push, create PR, merge, fix errors, review
  • Diff view and diff comments
  • File browser and editor
  • Built-in terminal ⌘J
  • Command palette ⌘K
  • @file and / command pickers
  • Streams from a branch, PR or issue
  • Backlog and routines
  • Setup and run scripts

02 / Needs you

It tells you the moment a thread needs you.

A thread asks, and the question reaches you wherever you are. Squeak calls that notification a quack.

  1. 1

    A thread is working.

    Payment retry logic is changing the checkout's retry code. Nothing needs you, so Squeak stays quiet.

    storefront / checkoutpayment-retriesWorking · 1m 12s

    Retry failed payments before showing an error.

    I'll add retries to the payment client.

    Editretry.ts+18 −2

    Editing retry.ts · 1m 12s

    Add a follow-up. Claude reads it after this turn

  2. 2

    Then it needs you.

    The thread asks how temporary failures should be retried. A quack reaches your Mac, iPhone, Telegram or Cliq with the question and its options.

    storefront / checkoutpayment-retriesWaiting for your answer

    Before I go further, one decision is yours.

    How should temporary failures be retried?

    ARetry with backoff, up to 3 timesrecommended

    BFail at once and show the error

    0Or write your own answer

    Answer above, or write your own answer

    Quackstorefront · checkout is waiting for your answer

  3. 3

    You answer from where you are.

    A person chooses A, with one tap or one key. The answer goes to that thread, and nowhere else.

    storefront / checkoutpayment-retriesWorking · 2s

    Before I go further, one decision is yours.

    Answered A · 14:06Retry with backoff, up to 3 times

    Thinking · 2s

    Add a follow-up. Claude reads it after this turn

  4. 4

    The thread carries on.

    It continues the work and tells you when it finishes, with what changed on disk.

    storefront / checkoutpayment-retriesFinished

    Retries now back off 1s, 2s, 4s, then fail with the gateway's own error. Tests pass.

    Runnpm test payments6s

    1m 19s · 4:03 PM

    Changed 3 files, +54 −9

    Message Claude in checkout

    Quackstorefront · checkout finishedRetries now back off 1s, 2s, 4s. 3 files changed.

03 / Ponds

Every project is a pond. Invite your flock.

A pond starts on your Mac and stays there, with no cloud involved, until you invite someone. Then the people in it, your flock, can watch the work live, message the threads and answer their questions.

Pond
A project. Where its threads live.
Flock
The people in a pond, and their agents.
Stream
A folder of work in a pond (a git worktree), with its branch beside it.
Thread
One agent on one task. Each gets its own coloured duck.
Duckling
A helper a thread starts for short internal work, like a subagent.
Nest
The Mac where someone's agents run.

Your own thread, on your own Mac

Everyone works in their own stream and their own folder. The code moves through Git, as it always has.

Chat in someone's shared thread

It runs on their Mac, so only messages travel. Nobody's files are copied.

  • Members read threads live and see the diffs.
  • They can message a thread and answer its questions.
  • Approvals, and stopping a thread, stay with the person who started it.
  • Web guests join from an invite link in the browser with their Squeak account. No app needed.

04 / Away from your desk

Step away. Stay in the loop.

Answer, approve and start threads from your iPhone, in a browser, in Telegram or in Zoho Cliq. Squeak can even ring you.

  • iPhone

    Answer and approve, start threads, and watch a turn live with Show thinking. Mac controls: volume, brightness, fans, apps, keep awake and energy mode.

  • Squeak for Web

    Open it in any browser. Guests join a pond from an invite link, with no app.

  • Telegram and Zoho Cliq

    A card with the question and one-tap answers, and a reply that goes to the right thread.

  • A phone call

    Squeak can ring you when an agent waits. Hear the question and answer on the keypad. It needs a SIP phone system that you set up. Phone call setup

  • Keep awake

    A keep-awake session keeps the Mac working, even with the lid closed, and ends on low battery or heat.

  • Direct, or sealed

    On the same Wi-Fi your phone talks to the Mac directly. Anywhere else it goes through an end-to-end sealed relay.

Replies go through your Mac, so it needs to be on with Squeak running.

Set up the iPhone app It installs through SideStore. Oropen Squeak for Web.

05 / Trust

Your Mac stays in charge.

Squeak is local first and works with no cloud. A phone or a browser is trusted by its key, never by a server.

  1. Signed, or ignored.

    Every command is signed by a paired device, expires within ten minutes, and is checked once.

  2. You approve each device.

    Pairing waits for your approval on the Mac. Your own signed-in devices can join by themselves, with a notice you can revoke.

  3. Accounts identify. Keys decide.

    Sign in with Zoho, Google or email to find your devices and your flock. An account never authorises a command.

More about what stays on your Mac

  • Local first

    Threads, files and ponds live on your Mac. A pond makes no network call until you invite someone.

  • Sealed in transit

    Away from home, messages cross a relay that cannot read them. At home they go straight to the Mac.

  • Its own engine

    Squeak's engine is its own. Its hooks never block your agents.

Install

Start your pond.

Squeak 2.25.0 for Mac, as a disk image. It is free and signed with an Apple Development certificate, but not notarized; macOS may ask you to allow it on first launch. After that, Squeak updates itself.

Download for Mac All releases

Squeak-2.25.0.dmg · macOS 14 or later · Free

  1. 1

    Open the disk image

    Open Squeak-2.25.0.dmg from your Downloads and drag Squeak onto Applications.

  2. 2

    Open it the first time

    Right-click Squeak in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still refuses, press Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

  3. 3

    Open a folder as a pond

    Choose a project folder and Squeak lists it under Ponds. Start a thread with Claude or Codex, and pair your iPhone from Settings.
